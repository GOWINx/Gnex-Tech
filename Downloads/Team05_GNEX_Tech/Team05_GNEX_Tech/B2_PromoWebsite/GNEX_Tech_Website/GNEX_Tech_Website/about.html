About GNEX Tech

We make security something people do, not something they sit through.

Our mission

To give every logistics worker the instincts to see a cyber threat, spot what is wrong and stop it, through immersive, game-based training that managers can measure.

Vision

A logistics industry in which the person on the dock is the strongest security control, not the weakest link.

Values

Practical: learning by doing. Honest: we state our limits. Secure: we protect our users’ data as carefully as we teach others to.

Who we are

GNEX Tech is Team 05 on the CET257 Enterprise Project (University of Sunderland, delivered at ISMT College, Kathmandu).

How we work

Iterative, evidence-led, client-first

We work in short iterations. Each version is shown to the client, the feedback becomes our next requirements, and automated tests make sure nothing we fixed breaks again. Weekly team meetings are minuted, and decisions are recorded.

Corporate identity

Black #0B0B0C for trust and focus, green #2FA35C for “safe” and growth, with product accents in neon green #30F5A0 and pink #FF3D9A. The X in our logo is cut by a green stroke: the moment a threat is stopped.
