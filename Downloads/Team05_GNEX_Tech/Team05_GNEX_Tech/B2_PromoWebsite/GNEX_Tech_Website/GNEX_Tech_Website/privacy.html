Privacy notice

Your privacy

This website

The GNEX Tech website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not store anything you type. The contact form only prepares an e-mail in your own e-mail app; you decide whether to send it.

Cyber Warehouse Defender (the product)

When an organisation uses our product, it is the data controller and GNEX Tech acts as its processor. We store only what the training needs: name, work e-mail, optional employee ID and department, and training results. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and are never visible to administrators.

Your rights

Learners can download all of their data from Profile & data → Download my data (UK GDPR right of access). For correction or deletion, contact your organisation’s administrator or e-mail us.

Security

HTTPS everywhere, database row-level security, an append-only audit log, account lock-out and automatic sign-out. See the B4 Security Report for details.
