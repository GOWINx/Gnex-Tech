The problem

Attackers do not break in through the firewall. They walk in through people.

Modern warehouses run on scanners, shared PCs, Wi-Fi and partner portals. One clicked link, a password on a sticky note or a found USB stick can stop shipments for days. Yearly slide-deck training is forgotten in a week, and managers have no proof of who understood it.

Our solution

Learning by doing, with evidence built in.

Cyber Warehouse Defender turns security awareness into short, realistic missions: triage phishing e-mails, harden passwords, find rogue access points, contain ransomware. Every decision is explained, every result is recorded, and every learner who passes earns a verifiable GNEX Tech certificate.

How it works

From sign-in to certificate in four steps

Sign in

Staff create an account or are invited. Admins and learners each see their own space.

Play missions

Explore the 3D warehouse, open hotspots and solve real security tasks against the clock.

Learn from every choice

Instant explanations, grades from S to F and a dashboard that shows what to practise next.

Prove it

Pass all six missions to claim a certificate with a QR code anyone can verify.
