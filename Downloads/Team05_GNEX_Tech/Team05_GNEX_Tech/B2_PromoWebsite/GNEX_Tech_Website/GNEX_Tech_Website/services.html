Services

Security awareness that fits how warehouses work.

One platform, four ways we help: the training product itself, scenarios built around your site, a smooth roll-out and the reporting your auditors expect.

🎮

1. Cyber Warehouse Defender platform

Hosted, browser-based training for every member of staff.

  • Six missions: awareness, endpoints, network, ransomware, supply chain, Operation Blackout
  • Learner dashboard, history, achievements and certificate
  • Admin console with analytics, account management and audit log
  • Works on existing PCs; offline demo mode for classrooms
⚙

2. Custom scenarios and content

Training that uses your procedures and your language.

  • Your organisation name in the 3D world, briefings and results
  • Your incident-reporting procedure on every mission
  • Your own question bank by topic, managed in the admin console
  • New missions on request (social engineering is next on our roadmap)
🚀

3. Setup and onboarding

Live in an afternoon, not a quarter.

  • Supabase project and Netlify hosting set up with you (about 20 minutes)
  • Two administrators trained; pass mark and settings agreed
  • Step-by-step setup guide and operating instructions
  • Launch message templates for your staff
📊

4. Reporting and compliance evidence

Know who is trained and where the gaps are.

  • Organisation knowledge gaps and accuracy by department
  • Learners-needing-attention list
  • CSV exports and a full report with certificate IDs
  • Append-only audit log of admin actions and certificates
